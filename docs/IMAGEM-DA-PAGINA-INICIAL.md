# Imagem da página inicial

Atualização de 03/11/2026: substituição da ilustração individual por uma imagem realista de um grupo de pessoas negras com diferentes tons de pele, conforme solicitado pelo usuário.

Gerada pela ferramenta integrada de imagens, em modo de geração de nova imagem. Pessoas fictícias; a imagem não retrata participantes reais da ação. A legenda da página informa que a imagem é ilustrativa e gerada por IA.

Arquivos usados no site:

- `assets/imagens/hero-grupo-negritude.webp` — 1200 × 1200 pixels.
- `assets/imagens/hero-grupo-negritude-720.webp` — 720 × 720 pixels.

O PNG original foi preservado na pasta de imagens geradas do Codex. Os WebP são derivados de tamanho e compressão, sem alteração do conteúdo da imagem.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square editorial hero photograph for a Brazilian community racial literacy website. Primary request: a highly realistic, warm, respectful group portrait of four fictional Black Brazilian adults with visibly varied Black skin tones, from light brown through medium and deep dark brown, together in a relaxed conversation in a welcoming educational community space. Two women and two men, adults roughly 20 to 55, varied natural Afro-textured hair, short curls and braids; distinct individual faces and natural expressions. Show joyful attentiveness and connection, not a posed diversity stock-photo lineup. Composition: square 1:1, cohesive close group framed from the waist/chest up around a small table, every face fully visible within the central 75 percent, with comfortable headroom and margin so website cropping never cuts a head. Mix their positions naturally, not ordered by skin shade. Background softly out of focus: cream walls, a leafy plant, a quiet warm wood detail. Contemporary everyday clothes in muted green, cream and terracotta. Lighting: gentle natural window daylight with neutral color rendering, accurate nuanced skin tones, realistic pores, hair texture, and unretouched appearance. Editorial documentary photography with subtle depth of field, calm and welcoming. Constraints: no text, no logos, no school emblems, no watermarks, no infographic, no cartoon, no painting, no waxy skin, no exaggerated smiles, no orange color grading, no split panels. People are fictional and must not resemble any named public figure. Generate only the photographic image, not a website mockup.
